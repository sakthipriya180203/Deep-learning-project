Advanced Time Series Forecasting with Attention

Dataset:
Synthetic series with trend, seasonality and noise.

Models:
1) Baseline LSTM
2) Attention LSTM

Attention:
Learns importance weights over time steps.
Helps focus on recent/seasonal values.

Hyperparameter tuning:
Optuna used to optimize batch size.

Metrics:
RMSE, MAE, MAPE computed on test set.

Result:
Attention model performed better than baseline with lower error.
Attention visualization shows higher weights on recent timesteps.
