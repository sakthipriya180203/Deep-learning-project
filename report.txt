Dataset:
Synthetic multivariate time series with trend and two seasonalities.

Models:

1. Baseline LSTM
2. LSTM with Attention

Tuning:
Optuna optimized units and learning rate.

Results:
Attention model achieved lower RMSE and MAE than baseline.

Attention Interpretation:
Heatmap shows higher focus on recent timesteps and seasonal peaks.

Conclusion:
Attention improves forecasting accuracy by learning temporal importance.
